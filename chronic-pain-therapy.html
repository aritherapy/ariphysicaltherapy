Chronic Pain

Chronic Pain Physical Therapy in Bakersfield

Get to the root of persistent pain with movement therapy, hands-on care, and strategies for a more comfortable, active life.

Request a Consultation

Living with chronic pain is exhausting; you deserve more than just “managing” it. At ARI Physical Therapy, our one-on-one approach addresses the root causes of persistent pain through movement therapy, manual techniques, and patient education, helping you build long-term strategies for a more comfortable, active life.

What we treat

✓Low back & neck pain
✓Fibromyalgia & widespread pain
✓Headaches & TMJ dysfunction
✓Sciatica & nerve pain
✓Arthritis & joint pain
✓Pain education & self-management

What to expect

1
A thorough evaluation

Your first visit (about 45–60 minutes) includes a hands-on assessment and a discussion of your history and goals.

2
A personalized plan

Hands-on care plus targeted, progressive exercise built around you, never a one-size-fits-all program.

3
Real, lasting progress

We track your improvement and guide you back to the activities that matter, with strategies to keep the gains.

No referral needed to start. In California, you can see a physical therapist directly for up to 12 visits or 45 days, so you can begin right away. Some insurance plans still require a referral for coverage; call us at (661) 282-8584 and we'll help you check.
