Sports Rehab

Sports Injury Physical Therapy in Bakersfield

One-on-one rehab and return-to-sport care. Get back to peak performance safely, and stay in the game.

Request a Consultation

Whether you're a weekend warrior or a competitive athlete, a sports injury can sideline you fast. At ARI Physical Therapy, we specialize in getting athletes back to peak performance safely, combining sport-specific, one-on-one rehab with injury-prevention strategies to keep you in the game long-term.

What we treat

✓ACL, MCL & rotator cuff injuries
✓Sprains, strains & tendinopathies
✓Knee, shoulder & ankle injuries
✓Overuse & repetitive-strain injuries
✓Return-to-sport programs
✓Performance & injury prevention

What to expect

1
A thorough movement evaluation

Your first visit (about 45–60 minutes) includes a hands-on assessment of strength, mobility, and the mechanics behind your injury.

2
A sport-specific rehab plan

Hands-on treatment plus targeted, progressive exercise, built around your sport and your goals rather than a generic protocol.

3
A confident return to sport

We use return-to-sport testing so you go back when you're truly ready, stronger and less likely to re-injure.

No referral needed to start. In California, you can see a physical therapist directly for up to 12 visits or 45 days, so you can begin recovery right away. Some insurance plans still require a referral for coverage; call us at (661) 282-8584 and we'll help you check.
