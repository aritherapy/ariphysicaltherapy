Wellness

Wellness & Fitness Programs in Bakersfield

Stay moving, strong, and injury-free for the long term: fall prevention, fitness, and maintaining the gains you made in therapy.

Request a Consultation

Great health doesn't stop when your formal therapy ends. At ARI Physical Therapy, our wellness programs keep you moving, strong, and injury-free for the long term, whether you're focused on fall prevention, general fitness, or maintaining the gains you made in therapy.

Our programs

  • Fall prevention for older adults
  • Injury prevention & movement screening
  • Strength & conditioning programs
  • Post-discharge wellness maintenance
  • Balance & mobility training
  • Personalized fitness coaching

What to expect

  • A thorough evaluationYour first visit (about 45–60 minutes) includes a hands-on assessment and a discussion of your history and goals.
  • A personalized planHands-on care plus targeted, progressive exercise built around you (never a one-size-fits-all program).
  • Real, lasting progressWe track your improvement and guide you back to the activities that matter, with strategies to keep the gains.

You don't need to be a current patient. Our wellness programs are open to anyone looking to stay strong, mobile, and active. Call us at (661) 282-8584 to find the right program for you.

Why patients choose ARI

Programs are led one-on-one and in person at our Bakersfield clinic, which is at 8200 Stockdale Hwy, Suite B2, in the Town & Country Village center next to Trader Joe's. Our team speaks English, Spanish, and Hindi. Call us to ask about program options and pricing.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to be a physical therapy patient to join?
No. Our wellness programs are open to anyone who wants to stay strong, mobile, and injury-free, whether or not you've been a therapy patient with us.
What kinds of programs do you offer?
Fall prevention, injury-prevention and movement screening, strength and conditioning, and maintenance programs for people finishing formal therapy. Call us and we'll match you to the right fit.
Are wellness programs covered by insurance?
Wellness and general fitness programs are typically not billed to insurance. Call us at (661) 282-8584 and we'll explain the program and pricing options.
How do I get started?
Call us at (661) 282-8584 or request a consultation online, and we'll help you choose the right program for your goals.
